About the List

Clark’s Take on the TIME100 AI 2026 List

TIME Magazine views artificial intelligence not as a novelty, but as a critical, knowledge-seeking technology that is actively reshaping society. To capture the full scope of this rapidly changing landscape, the TIME100 AI list highlights key figures in the stories from a wide range of diverse, evolving perspectives on the technology’s impact. The profiles are informed by dozens of first-person interviews conducted with industry leaders, researchers, policymakers, and advocates. This included in the year’s list reflects “TIME’s commitment to covering the most consequential story of our time.”
